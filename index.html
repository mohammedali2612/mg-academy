A Little Place for Big Dreams!

At MG Academy, we believe every child possesses unique creativity waiting to be unlocked. We provide a warm, encouraging, and colorful sanctuary where little hands learn techniques and big minds build lifelong confidence.

Our Mission

To ignite children's imaginations through structured, enjoyable learning programs in fine arts, expressive dance, and foundational mental skills. We ensure every child receives dedicated individual guidance, nurturing creative thinking alongside technical excellence.

  • Fostering joyful self-expression without fear of judgment
  • Personalized attention in small, supportive batches
  • Building fine motor skills, focus, and artistic discipline

Our Vision

To be the most loved creative learning community where every student discovers their inner artist, takes pride in their creative journey, and carries forward the discipline, grace, and confidence needed to shine in school and in life.

  • Creating future artists, performers, and holistic thinkers
  • Celebrating student achievements on stage and gallery showcases
  • Empowering young minds with both cultural roots & modern flair

Dedicated Dance Classes

From classical heritage to energetic modern rhythms, our dance studio offers 4 distinct styles to build poise, flexibility, musicality, and stage confidence.

Scroll to explore styles · Stacked Deck

Bharatanatyam

"Sacred grace, rhythmic footwork, and timeless cultural poise."

Traditional Indian classical dance focusing on foundational Adavus (footwork), delicate Hastha Mudras (hand gestures), Abhinaya (facial expressions), and rhythmic Salangai. Promotes discipline, balance, flexibility, and stage confidence.

2 Days / Week Ages 4 – 15 Yrs Salangai Pooja & Arangetram

Western Dance

"Dynamic beats, modern footwork, and high-energy stage performance."

High-energy modern routines that build physical coordination, stamina, and musicality. Kids groove to upbeat international tracks while mastering crisp footwork, isolations, and synchronized group choreography.

2 Days / Week Ages 5 – 16 Yrs Agility & Group Synchronization

Free Style

"Creative movement, joyful freedom, and individual rhythm expression."

Creative, expressive movement that frees children from rigid boundaries. Blends elements of hip-hop, lyrical flow, and contemporary pop routines, allowing children to develop their own personal groove and rhythm.

2 Days / Week Ages 4 – 16 Yrs Freestyle Flow & Confidence

Tamil Folk Dance

"Pulsating drums, authentic folk traditions, and festive joy."

Vibrant, energetic traditional folk styles including Karagattam, Oyilattam, and Kummi steps that celebrate regional culture, pulsating drum beats, festive joy, and authentic community teamwork.

2 Days / Week Ages 5 – 15 Yrs Festive Beats & Stage Shows
